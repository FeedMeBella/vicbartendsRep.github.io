From: Winter
Date: 24-03-2026
Subject: Hi, I'm Winter.

I wonder what things will be written here.

Maybe I'll write beautiful things. Mourning isn't all that moves my heart, though it is that which fuels me to write the most. I fear I'll leave you only with my sorrows, and "i have to much time on my hands to think about stuff" problems. Surely one of you will come by with more endearing words? I'll trust you with that. Or who knows, perhaps other patrons and strangers will write on here; voices we didn't create ourselves, perspectives different than our own.

That would be nice.

This page was pretty much my idea, and I know I'm free to do whatever I want with it, but I just hope this place isn't too depressing a year from now. Things have been getting worse, and beautiful thoughts seem more out of place than ever in the midst of it all and... whatever. I could spend some time to make this first entry all pretty and well written, but I don't wanna start it that way.

Hi, I'm Winter. I'm fucking tired and forever yearning, and full of big feelings. I hope you like it here.
