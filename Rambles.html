From: Autumn
Date: 05-05-2026
Subject: Art block and why do a thing

Ever get that feeling that something is just wrong? Like a tingle before a big headache, or an anxiety setting in upon hearing a voice, only to be greeted by someone you wished not to see? It's a funny thing, how these feelings are quicker than our understanding. I call the right side of my brain Ruth, and I like to think it's her, warning me when anything is going to happen; like a diet, flaw-ridden, less amazing version of a guardian angel.

It's fitting, really. I've made friends in my head ever since I was a kid, and I never thought to make them in any way external to me. Maybe I wasn't creative enough. Maybe I was scared they'd grow in a different direction than I. It'd be cause for complaint if I had ever gotten any troubles out of it, but Ruth and the others treat me well. Anyway.

Lately I've had this gnawing feeling, like I'm about to be pushed off a cliff, like something foreign is growing inside me, like a haze is covering my eyes, dooming me to get further and further away from my path. I wonder if other people feel the same when dealing with art block.

You spend months learning a new creative skill, and you read about this thing everyone feels and all, and you wonder how it'll be like. Wonder if maybe you'll be immune to it. In my experience, it's different depending on what flavor of art you're making too. With music I felt like anything new and original was out of reach, no matter how much I tried. With writing, it felt like a lack of interest in the subject matter, a fizzling of passion that left me no heart with which to work. And with visual art... it's a fear. As if a canvas were going to eat me whole the moment I touched it, as if I were back at the start after forgetting all I've learned, and the moment I try and actually do something, it'll confirm my demise. And when I do try to prove myself wrong, I am unable to focus, and the fear proliferates.

I got my sketchbook out today. I laid it across my desk next to my markers and pencils and pens, and I told myself that at some point today I would do a thing. Any thing. I have yet to do so, and it's frustrating. And I know it's just a hobby, but I've come to put so much weight on this one thing. 4 years ago I didn't even do this, and now it's this pillar of my identity.

In Cosmos, Carl Sagan wrote that "we are a way for the universe to know itself," and I hold on to that quote so, so dearly. And I already learn so much of the cosmos in my professional career, but it isn't enough. Art seems like a window to a whole new universe of things to see and experience and to know...

If only I can grasp it. It's easy to fall into bad habits and to burn your dopamine receptors or whatever it is that happens to me when I can't do a single thing for weeks. It's easy to not do the things you should do, and instead pick up a brain-dead consumption-based thing that won't fix my feelings of fear and anxiety but will numb the pain, and I hate it. I don't want to be numb.

I'll try again tomorrow. And if that doesn't work out, I'll try the next day and the day after; until I can go back to feeling whole.

Subject: Hi, I'm Winter.

I wonder what things will be written here.

Maybe I'll write beautiful things. Mourning isn't all that moves my heart, though it is that which fuels me to write the most. I fear I'll leave you only with my sorrows, and "i have to much time on my hands to think about stuff" problems. Surely one of you will come by with more endearing words? I'll trust you with that. Or who knows, perhaps other patrons and strangers will write on here; voices we didn't create ourselves, perspectives different than our own.

That would be nice.

This page was pretty much my idea, and I know I'm free to do whatever I want with it, but I just hope this place isn't too depressing a year from now. Things have been getting worse, and beautiful thoughts seem more out of place than ever in the midst of it all and... whatever. I could spend some time to make this first entry all pretty and well written, but I don't wanna start it that way.

Hi, I'm Winter. I'm fucking tired and forever yearning, and full of big feelings. I hope you like it here.
